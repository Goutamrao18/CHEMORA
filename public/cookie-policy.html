Cookie & Storage Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective: Immediately

Note for Site Operator: Draft: review with a lawyer. Documents cookies, local storage, and service providers (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Supabase).

1. Purpose of Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies and browser local storage are essential mechanisms placed on your computer or mobile device to maintain your secure authenticated researcher session, preserve chemical progress across sessions, and remember interface preferences.

2. Cookies & Storage Technologies Used by CHEMORA

Name / Key Type Duration Purpose
chemora_session / __Host-chemora_session HTTP Cookie (httpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) 7 days Maintains secure authenticated researcher login session. Inaccessible to client JavaScript.
chemora_cookie_consent Local Storage (Essential) Persistent Stores your cookie consent preferences and choices.
chemora_streak_data Local Storage (Functional) Persistent Caches daily learning streaks and challenge milestones for offline study.

3. Third-Party Processing & Storage

CHEMORA integrates trusted cloud infrastructure to power its features:

  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud database and asset storage for user profiles and learning progress.
  • Google Gemini & OpenRouter: Process chemistry prompts, equation inquiries, and uploaded homework photos ephemerally to provide explanations. No marketing tracking cookies are transmitted.
  • Upstash Redis: High-performance sliding window rate limiting to guard endpoints against denial-of-service without tracking individual personal identities.

4. Managing and Clearing Preferences

You can clear cookies and storage keys anytime via your browser settings or log out of CHEMORA to revoke the active 7-day session token and clear the authentication cookie.
