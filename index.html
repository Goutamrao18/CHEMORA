HELP & TRUST

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers based on what CHEMORA currently provides.

What is CHEMORA?

CHEMORA is a chemistry learning platform with a knowledge library, guided experiments, progressive challenges and an AI chemistry tutor.

Can I track my learning progress?

Yes. Challenge and learning progress is stored in your browser, and signed-in accounts can store profile and account-related progress through Supabase.

Does CHEMORA replace a real laboratory?

No. The virtual tools are for learning and simulation. Follow your school or laboratory's safety rules for real experiments.

How does CHEMORA AI work?

The site sends your question to a server endpoint, which uses OpenRouter to generate a chemistry-focused response.

Is my account required?

Most public learning pages can be explored without signing in. The Account page provides optional profile and progress features.
