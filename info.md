## 1. What is Chemora? (Platform Purpose & Overview)

**Chemora** is a modern, high-performance, AI-assisted interactive chemistry education and laboratory simulation suite. Designed for high school, undergraduate, and advanced chemistry researchers, Chemora bridges the gap between abstract chemical theories and hands-on laboratory experimentation.

### Core Educational Modules:
1. **Interactive Laboratory Chamber (Home)**:
   - Daily Chemistry Spotlight, real-time chemical reaction previews, and quick-launch chambers.
   - Dynamic dark-matter visual theme with fluid gradient waves and responsive navigation.
2. **Apparatus & Reaction Knowledge Library (`/know`)**:
   - Extensive interactive reference covering laboratory glassware, synthesis setups, and chemical reagents.
   - **Interactive 3D Molecular Engine**: Integrated `3Dmol.js` and `SMILES Drawer` allowing interactive rotational inspection of organic molecules, proteins, and crystal lattices retrieved dynamically via the PubChem API.
   - Full periodic table with thermodynamic, orbital, and electronegativity telemetry.
3. **Chemistry Challenge Arena (`/challenge`)**:
   - **100 Levels** spanning General Chemistry, Stoichiometry, Thermodynamics, Kinetics, Equilibrium, Organic Synthesis, Spectroscopy, and Biochemistry.
   - **1,000 Curated Questions** with LaTeX equation rendering (via KaTeX), immediate analytical feedback, XP rewards, and streak tracking.
4. **Guided Laboratory Procedures (`/experiments`)**:
   - **20 Interactive Laboratory Procedures** with step-by-step practical steps, hazard diamonds (NFPA 704), procedure checklists, and high-definition video streaming.
5. **Learning Progress & Achievements (`/progress`)**:
   - Daily streak tracking, mastery rank tiers (Novice Alchemist $\rightarrow$ Grand Chemist), XP accumulation, and detailed accuracy breakdowns by topic.
6. **Researcher Workspace & Account (`/account`)**:
   - **Zero-LocalStorage Architecture**: User authentication, profiles, and laboratory progress synchronize securely with a Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL database.
   - Custom avatar cropping with canvas rendering and secure storage bucket isolation.
7. **Chemora AI Chemistry Assistant (`AIAssistant.tsx`)**:
   - Gemini-powered chemical reasoning assistant capable of explaining reaction mechanisms, balancing complex redox reactions, and guiding students through homework queries without solving tests directly.
8. **Administrative Command Center (`/admin`)**:
   - Multi-role RBAC management (Super Admin, DevOps, Content Moderator).
   - Two-Factor Authentication via HMAC Email One-Time Passwords (OTP).
   - Real-time review moderation, content health audits, video streaming latency monitors, and tamper-evident audit logging.
   - **DevOps & Cloud Security Operations Panel**: CI/CD deployment rollback, Edge CDN cache purging, live security headers inspector, database/compute telemetry, and RBAC matrix.

### The 4 Core Principles:
- **1. Free Educational Use**: Chemora Lab is a free, AI-assisted platform for supplementary learning. Simulations are for informational purposes only.
- **2. AI Disclaimer**: Content generated by AI may contain errors. Students are guided to cross-verify data with standard academic textbooks.
- **3. Secure Login Data**: Logins are completely optional. Authentication data (email and credentials) is processed securely via Supabase and stored in encrypted cloud databases.
- **4. Student Protection**: Zero student activity tracking for advertisements, zero sale of student data, and zero invasive third-party tracking cookies. Users can request immediate deletion of their account and all saved data at any time.

#### 6. System-Wide Rate Limit Hardening & Elimination of False-Positive 429 / 503 Errors
- **Problem**: Users and students were experiencing "Rate limit exceeded" (HTTP 429) and "Service temporarily unavailable" (HTTP 503) errors during regular navigation, rapid chemistry assistant queries, and password reset flows.
- **Root Cause Analysis**:
  1. **Double Rate Limiting on AI Assistant**: The AI chemistry assistant endpoint was throttled simultaneously by Express `aiRateLimiter` (previously 20 req/min) and Upstash Redis rate limiters (`anonMinLimiter` previously 5 req/min). Students asking consecutive follow-ups hit limits within 30 seconds.
  2. **Premature Client Failure on 429**: `src/utils/fetchWithRetry.ts` previously only retried on 502, 503, and 504 status codes. A single transient 429 immediately broke UI actions without honoring `Retry-After`.
  3. **Fail-Closed 503 in Production**: `authRateLimiter`, `checkPasswordResetRateLimit`, and `adminLoginRateLimiter` had a hard fail-closed block that immediately returned HTTP 503 if Upstash Redis credentials were not configured in production environments.
  4. **Strict Shared-IP Windows**: Cloud Run and AI Studio container environments share reverse-proxy ingress IPs (`127.0.0.1` or gateway NAT), causing single-IP bucket starvation.
- **Architectural Solutions**:
  1. **Intelligent Client-Side Retry on 429 (`fetchWithRetry.ts`)**:
     - Added HTTP `429` to `retryOnStatus` (`[429, 502, 503, 504]`).
     - Added parsing for the standard `Retry-After` header with a safety cap (up to 4000ms), and raised maximum retry attempts from 2 to 3 with exponential backoff and jitter. Client calls now transparently auto-recover.
  2. **Relaxed Express API Rate Limiters (`server/middleware/rateLimit.ts`)**:
     - `apiRateLimiter`: Increased from 120 to 600 req/min (10 req/sec per IP).
     - `databaseRateLimiter`: Increased from 60 to 300 req/min (5 req/sec per IP) and added bypass for read-only health checks (`/status`, `/diagnostics`).
     - `aiRateLimiter`: Increased from 20 to 60 req/min.
     - `authRateLimiter`: Increased from 10 to 50 attempts/15 min.
  3. **High-Capacity AI Quotas (`api/assistant.js`)**:
     - Increased global daily cap (`AI_DAILY_CAP`) from 1,000 to 10,000 requests.
     - Relaxed anonymous limits: 5 $\rightarrow$ 30 req/min; 30 $\rightarrow$ 150 req/day.
     - Relaxed authenticated user limits: 20 $\rightarrow$ 60 req/min; 200 $\rightarrow$ 600 req/day.
     - Updated in-memory fallback limits to match (30/min, 150/day anon; 60/min, 600/day user).
     - Replaced non-existent model aliases (`gemini-3.5-flash`, `gemini-3.8-flash`) with verified production models (`gemini-2.5-flash`, `gemini-2.5-pro`, `gemini-2.0-flash`, `gemini-1.5-flash`).
     - Increased per-model timeout ceiling from 4500ms to 8000ms.
  4. **Resilient In-Memory Fallback for 503 Prevention**:
     - Replaced fail-closed 503 blocks in `server/middleware/rateLimit.ts`, `server/routes/account.ts`, and `server/routes/admin.ts` with graceful sliding-window memory fallbacks.
     - Relaxed `adminAssistantLimiter` from 10 to 30 req/min.
