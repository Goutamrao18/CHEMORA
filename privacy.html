2. Age Restrictions & Parental Consent

CHEMORA is designed for students and researchers. Users must be at least 13 years old to create an account. For users aged between 13 and 17, account registration requires parental or legal guardian consent and supervision. By creating an account, you affirm that you satisfy these minimum age conditions.

3. Data Collection & Processing

We process the following categories of data strictly for educational functionality:

  • Authentication & Profile Data: Email address and hashed password credentials managed via Supabase Auth. Display names and chosen avatar identifiers are stored in the user profile table.
  • Learning Progress & Diagnostics: Challenge levels completed, quiz answers, accuracy statistics, laboratory experiment completions, and XP counters stored in Supabase database tables with Row-Level Security.
  • AI Assistant Queries & Homework Photos: Questions and chemistry homework photos uploaded to the AI assistant are processed by Google Gemini and OpenRouter (using selected model providers) solely to generate scientific explanations, chemical equations, and safety guidance. Questions and photos are processed ephemerally and are never sold or used to train public models.
  • Cloud Storage: Media assets and user data are securely stored in Supabase Storage and databases protected by encryption at rest and in transit.

4. Cookies & Browser Storage

CHEMORA uses the following limited cookies and storage mechanisms:

  • chemora_session (or __Host-chemora_session): An httpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax authentication cookie with a 7-day lifespan used exclusively to authenticate your requests to the API. It cannot be accessed by client-side JavaScript.
  • localStorage: Used for offline caching of challenge questions and offline PWA state so you can study without network connectivity.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Technical Infrastructure

  • Google Gemini API: Processes chemical questions and multimodal vision analysis of homework photos.
  • OpenRouter: Secondary proxy for AI scientific analysis and model inference.
  • Supabase: Primary cloud database, authentication engine, and asset storage.
  • Upstash Redis: High-performance in-memory RAM acceleration and sliding-window rate limiting infrastructure.
  • Vercel: Edge hosting, serverless execution, and global asset distribution.
  • AI Coding Architecture: Engineered through AI coding (vibe coding: Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT).

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have full ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account at any time:

  1. Sign in to your CHEMORA account.
  2. Navigate to your Account Dashboard.
  3. Under the Danger Zone section, select “Delete Account”.
  4. Re-enter your account password to confirm identity.
  5. Upon confirmation, the server permanently deletes your auth credentials, cascading and purging your researcher profile, learning progress, and review records from our databases immediately.

For additional privacy requests or data export inquiries, email us directly at chemora_lab@agentmail.to.
