CHEMORA

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

CHEMORA is an interactive chemistry learning platform dedicated to education, simulation, and scientific mastery. We prioritize student data privacy, transparency, and minimal data collection.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver educational tracking and account authentication:

  • Authentication Data: When registering, your email address and encrypted password credentials are managed securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Profile Data: Display name and chosen avatar stored in the database for personalized research profiles.
  • Progress & Learning Analytics: Completed challenge levels, quiz answers, accuracy rates, and experimental completions.
  • AI Assistant Queries: User prompts sent to the chemistry assistant are processed ephemerally to generate explanations and are never sold.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

CHEMORA uses browser storage (localStorage) for offline PWA progress caching and light/dark theme preferences. Authentication session tokens are stored securely in browser session cookies or local auth storage.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Provides encrypted authentication, database storage, and Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Vercel: Hosts serverless endpoints and static distribution with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can view, edit, or reset your profile credentials directly through your Account Dashboard. To request complete account and data removal, contact our administration team.
