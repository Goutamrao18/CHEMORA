Privacy Policy

What CHEMORA stores

  • Account information such as email and password authentication is handled by Supabase when you use the Account page.
  • Your optional profile name and avatar are stored in the Supabase profile/storage services used by the Account page.
  • Learning progress, challenge state, experiment progress and theme preference are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Questions sent to CHEMORA AI are sent to the site's server endpoint, which uses OpenRouter to generate a response. The current client sends the question and limited page context.

Third-party services

The site uses Supabase for authentication, database/profile data and storage. AI features use OpenRouter through server-side Vercel functions. Some fonts/icons are loaded from third-party CDNs.

Cookies and local storage

CHEMORA does not use Google Analytics or other analytics tracking. Browser local storage is used for site preferences and learning state. Supabase handles account authentication and database-backed features.

Security

Never place the OpenRouter secret key in client-side code. Keep it in Vercel environment variables. Review Supabase Row Level Security, storage policies, authentication settings, and your production domain before launch.

Contact

For questions about CHEMORA, this privacy notice, or your account, contact support@chemora.work.gd.
